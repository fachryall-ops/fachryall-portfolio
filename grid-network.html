Project Overview

Grid Network is a publisher that produces and distributes news, entertainment, and lifestyle content to tens of millions of readers in Indonesia. Grid Network (formerly known as Gramedia Majalah or Kompas Gramedia Group of Magazine) is a publishing company that produces and distributes news, entertainment, and lifestyle content to tens of millions of readers in Indonesia.

I started my first career at Grid Network and have been working for 3 years in the Marcomm Activation Department. As a designer in the activation team, I am responsible for creating event requirements that will run smoothly, both offline and online. I directly coordinate with project managers, brand communications, procurement, vendors, and other departments to fulfill the tasks. The high demand requires me to work quickly.

Events

ICMA 2020 - Grid Voice & Grid Story Factory

ICMA or Indonesia: Choice Marketing Awards is the first content marketing awards event in Indonesia. It is organized by Grid Story Factory and Grid Voice Kompas Gramedia. The awards are given as a form of appreciation for the performance and creativity of brands or institutions in Indonesia in implementing content marketing.

In 2020, this event had to be postponed due to the COVID-19 pandemic, but the design concept had already been created.

Design Collage

Key visual

The design already has a key visual that can be further used for other design needs. The design above is a poster implementation, which serves as the main poster and countdown/coming soon design.

Print

Several designs have been created for further processing, such as invitations and tickets. These designs still adhere to the previously created key visual. The invitation design uses an envelope, while the ticket design has two alternatives as it is still in the development stage. Other designs include signage and a photowall.

Local Corn, Presented by HP 2021 - HP x Tribun News

Local Corn is a local StartUp competition event, held in collaboration by Tribun News & HP Indonesia. In this project, I directly coordinate with the project manager and vendors in its execution, as there are several technical aspects that need to be adjusted due to the involvement of 3D live streaming, which requires accuracy in visualization and operation.

Kita Bangkit (Plasma of Kindness) - KG Media

KG Media, together with the Indonesian Red Cross (PMI) and Plasmahero.id, organized a convalescent plasma donation activity entitled "Plasma of Kindness." This activity was driven by the high number of Covid-19 cases in Indonesia at that time, which led to an increasing demand for blood bags and convalescent plasma therapy. However, the limited availability of these resources diminished their hopes of recovery.

Backdrop
The first hard production item is the backdrop for the event, where the content uses existing copy from the key visual. The backdrop is intended to stand out during the event.

Photobooth

Merchandise

The design material that requires significant production time is the merchandise, specifically a pouch for this event. The pouch design is kept simple to align with the production timeline and ensure production efficiency. Simplicity is preferred, utilizing two colors and incorporating the key visual.

Print

The ID card is a printed production item that can be quickly produced. With its small size and simple material, the design uses a harmonious key visual that already exists, ensuring easy readability.

The design for the preview only displays the committee members.

Another design that can be quickly produced, similar to the ID card, is the signage. Although signage has a larger size compared to the ID card, its production speed is still similar. Signage design utilizes A3 size or larger and sticker material applied to polyfoam.

CustomMAXI - Yamaha x Grid.Oto

CustomMAXI is a modification competition for Yamaha motorcycles in the MAXI category. This activity is a form of appreciation from Yamaha to all MAXI Owners and also provides a positive platform for MAXI Owners to showcase their own modified MAXI creations. This activity is carried out in collaboration with the publisher Grid.Oto.

Design

The design requirements for this event primarily involve print and hard production, and the design process is conducted in a hybrid manner, both online and offline.

Setup

As for the 3D design for the online live setup, the setup is located in a studio with a focus on branding on the screen and Yamaha motorcycles.
