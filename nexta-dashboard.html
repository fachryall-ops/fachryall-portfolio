My First Step

Project Brief

On this occasion, I wanted to explore my abilities as a UI/UX Designer. What exactly did I want to explore?

Challenge 1

Design the desktop UI of a dashboard used by customer service at a telecom provider to serve customers who visit the outlet. The dashboard should provide comprehensive information about customer account data and help customer service work efficiently.

Key information to display:

  • Customer Profile: Basic customer information such as name, customer number, address, contact number, and account status.
  • Active Package Information: Details of the package the customer is currently using, including data quota, remaining call time, and any additional services.
  • Usage History & Billing: Service usage data such as call, SMS, and internet data history, plus the customer’s monthly bill and payment status.

Challenge 2

Design the UI for the customer service flow of changing the service package for the following customer.

  • Customer Data Dashboard: The dashboard shows customer details, including the active package, usage history, and billing.
  • Package Change: Customer service changes the package from the active package information.
  • Select New Package: Customer service can choose a new package from the available list, with a comparison to the previous package.
  • Confirm Changes: A summary of the changes is shown and the customer is asked to confirm. Inform the customer if there are any additional fees.
  • Payment Process: If needed, payment is made through the available methods (credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
  • Notifications & New Package Status.
