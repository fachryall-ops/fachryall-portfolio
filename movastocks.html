Project Overview

As we continue to evolve in the digital age, creating an intuitive and engaging user experience is more crucial than ever. At MovaStocks, we’ve focused on designing a platform that not only meets the needs of modern investors but also elevates their experience with seamless navigation, real-time insights, and personalized event notifications. With user-centric design at the core, MovaStocks empowers users to make informed decisions, putting control directly in their hands—because it’s not just about the stocks you own, but the moves you make.

How Have I Started?

Project Brief

This project has been assigned as a test assignment for a company in the fintech industry. With a request to create a new brand design for “Event & Stock Details” for a stock trading app, this app is targeted at the younger generation.

Brand Name Research

Inspiration

  • Appealing Name: A name that is memorable, has a connection to the investment world, yet remains modern and appealing to the younger generation.
  • Relevance: Connecting the name with key features like "Event" and "Stock Details" to provide insight into the app's functionality.

Brand Names

  • TrendVest - Combines “Trend” and “Invest” to highlight a focus on the latest investment trends.
  • Stox+ - Simplifies the word “Stocks” with the addition of “+” to indicate added features.
  • EventTrade - Focuses on the event feature in trading.
  • NextGenTrade - Portrays an app designed for the younger generation.
  • MovaStocks - “Mova” and “Stocks” denote dynamism and movement in the stock market.
  • StockVibe - Blends “Stock” and “Vibe,” targeting the energy and enthusiasm of young investors.
  • Investly - A friendly and easy-to-remember name, rooted in the word “Invest.”

Logo

Following the research and survey, the name for the brand has been chosen, and a logo has also been created. The selected name is MovaStocks, applying colors that convey a dynamic, modern impression, representing technology, and showcasing trust as well as stability, appealing to the younger generation.

Simple Research

In this process, a simple method has been used to align with the project timeline.

User Persona

At this phase, I have developed a user persona to narrow down the focus of the app's usage and to identify issues based on common user behavior.

Persona Name

  • Name: Rizky Pratama
  • Age: 24 years old
  • Occupation: Fresh graduate in economics, working as a data analyst at a tech startup
  • Status: Single
  • Location: Jakarta, Indonesia
  • Income: Rp 7,000,000 - Rp 12,000,000 per month
  • Lifestyle: Active on social media, frequently attends seminars and workshops on finance and investment, enjoys exploring the latest fintech apps.

Goals

  • To learn and invest in the stock market to build a personal investment portfolio.
  • To find a platform that is user-friendly, informative, and provides up-to-date information on market trends and important events.
  • Wants to invest small amounts regularly and increase knowledge about the stock market.

Digital Behavior

  • Frequently uses mobile apps for daily activities, including finance and investment.
  • Prefers apps that are user-friendly, have a modern interface, and provide relevant notifications.
  • Often reads articles and follows influencers who discuss investing.

Pain Points

  • Complicated and difficult-to-use apps.
  • Lack of relevant, real-time information on important events in the stock market.
  • Difficulty finding apps that provide interactive features that align with the needs of the younger generation.

Technology Preferences

  • Prefers apps with a clean and intuitive design.
  • Values customizable features, like personalized event notifications and relevant investment suggestions.
  • Frequently uses community-based apps where he can discuss with other users.

Motivation

  • Wants to better understand the stock market and how to invest wisely.
  • Interested in apps that provide information on events that influence stock prices.
  • Looking for ways to get involved in investing without feeling overwhelmed by excessive data.

Benchmarking

By conducting a bit of benchmarking, I aimed to develop an effective marketing strategy for a trading app that appeals to the younger generation. This benchmarking has been conducted with the Stockbit app.

  • Community Focus: One of Stockbit's strengths is its strong community. MovaStock could add a similar feature where users can share insights related to events and stocks.
  • Modern User Interface: A simple yet appealing design, like Stockbit’s, can serve as inspiration.
  • Event-Driven Notifications: Focusing on notifications related to important events could add extra value to the MovaStock app.

High-Fidelity (Hi-Fi)

As the final stage, I have divided this into two sections: Design System and UI Design. Given the very short timeline, I have created a simple design system for this project, while still aiming for comprehensive and consistent design in the long term.

Design System

The simple design system in this project does not display all elements such as icons and shadow styles.

Color

The colors used for the MovaStocks app are chosen to ensure brand consistency. The selected colors are based on elements of stability, trust, and growth.

This color style is divided into several uses as follows:

  1. Primary: As the main color, it is central to the product branding and is often used in marketing for product sales.
  2. Secondary: These are supporting colors that provide contrast to the primary color and can be used for highlights or accents.
  3. Feedback: Used to convey information such as success, warning, caution, and error.
  4. Surface: Used as background and text colors.

Typography

The font used in MovaStocks' typography style is Inter. Using the Inter font in a fintech app helps create a consistent, comfortable, and professional experience, which is essential in building user trust in the financial sector.

The Inter font is widely used in fintech applications for several key reasons:

  1. High Readability: Inter is designed with a focus on readability, especially for small text. This makes it ideal for fintech applications, where users need to quickly and accurately read numerical information, small text, and data.
  2. Modern and Minimalist Design: This font has a clean and modern style, aligning with the innovative and user-friendly image that fintech companies often strive to project.
  3. Multiplatform Support: Inter is designed to perform well across various devices and information systems.
  4. Accessibility and Support for Multiple Characters: Inter supports a wide range of characters and symbols necessary in fintech applications, including currency signs and non-Latin languages.

UI Design

Based on the timeline and brief, this design does not encompass all features of the MovaStocks application, but it remains focused on the core features.

In the MovaStocks app, there is a main menu that supports users in staying up-to-date with stock developments. This main menu enhances user understanding and includes the following options:

  • Home
  • Market
  • Portfolio
  • Insight
  • Profile

Home Page

As the main page, it presents a brief overview of the app's key features. This aims to help users quickly filter the information they need.

On this page, several sections have been created to display important information for users. This information can be in the form of highlights or require action to view more details. The information sections include the following:

The Home Page will showcase highlights of the user's portfolio value in card format, along with “Deposit” and “Withdraw” actions to facilitate quick responses when necessary. There will also be a Portfolio section in a slider format, aimed at providing highlights of the user's portfolio updates.

MovaStocks includes a banner to attract new customers/users by featuring a referral banner after the portfolio section. Following that, there is a Watchlist section where users can monitor stock activities in real-time. Additionally, there is a Stocks section that lists updates related to stocks from companies categorized accordingly. This is intended for users who wish to add stocks, allowing them to view this list as recommendations or personal analysis.

To enhance insights and updates related to stock developments, an “Events” section and an “Articles” section have been added. This allows users to actively follow current trends and improve their understanding of stocks, making it one of the menus or features that can increase interest among new customers/users.

Market

In this menu, there are tabs for Watchlist and Stocks, where these two features are different yet interconnected.

As a menu displaying the list of Watchlist and Stocks, the Watchlist will provide a detailed list of the selected companies' stocks for monitoring their value activities. Users can add or remove companies they wish to track and prioritize their list of companies.

The Stocks tab will display the entire list of company stocks. This menu will feature filtering options for the displayed list, as well as filters based on categories in the search functionality, aimed at simplifying the search process and helping users determine which industries to invest in.

On the Market page, when users click on a company's logo or section, they will be directed to that company's detail page.

Portfolio

This page presents the details of the user's portfolio value, which includes a list of companies in which the user has invested.

One of the features displayed on this menu is the details of each company, allowing users to view and analyze the stock values directly. This aims to enable users to make immediate decisions regarding their investments.

In addition to displaying stock value data, users can also view the entire transaction history they have conducted. The transaction details include a filtering feature, enabling users to keep accurate records as needed.

Insight

Insight is one of the main menus in MovaStocks, serving as a source of education and providing updates on stock trends.

This page offers a wealth of educational resources, featuring Events, Community, and Articles. The Events menu provides a list of upcoming and past events, enhancing the connection between users and MovaStocks while offering a unique experience.

There is also a community banner that directs users to the Telegram app. This community aims to maintain and facilitate a platform for sharing and enhancing interactions among users. Additionally, there is an Articles menu that provides the latest updates from the stock market, which helps to boost users' interest in financial literacy.

The Events page displays a list of events that are upcoming, labeled as “Upcoming,” and those that have already occurred, labeled as “Finished.” These details are grouped in cards that are marked with a label indicating the type of event (Webinar, Workshop, Experience).

On the Upcoming page, there will be a list of scheduled events along with a bell button to remind users when the event is about to start. The Finished page will showcase a list of completed events, but it will only include webinars.

The Event Details page will appear when users click on a card. This page will display detailed information about the event, such as registration details, reminders, and recommendations for other upcoming events.

Profile

This menu only displays a single page, as it is not a priority in this project.

This menu allows users to manage their personalization and account privacy. The available options on this page include:

  • Referal Banner
  • Account
  • Security
  • Billing / Payments
  • Language
  • Settings
  • FAQ
  • Log Out

Success Expectations

I believe the success of the MovaStocks application heavily relies on a combination of relevant features, effective marketing strategies, and their ability to meet the needs and preferences of young investors in Indonesia. The prominent factors include:

  • Enhanced User Experience: The application focuses on an intuitive and user-friendly interface, making investment more accessible for the younger generation.
  • Education and Community: Providing a variety of educational resources and building an active community to help new users learn and grow as investors.
  • Promotions and Incentives: Utilizing promotions and incentives to attract new users and retain existing ones.
