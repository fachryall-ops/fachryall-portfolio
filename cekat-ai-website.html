01 · Overview

An AI product that needed a clearer story.

Cekat.AI is an AI chat omnichannel platform that helps businesses automate and analyze their customer conversations. The website had to do more than describe features: it needed to bring more people in and help them understand the product fast enough to try it.

Make Cekat.AI easy to understand, easy to trust, and easy to try.The goal behind every page

02 · The problem

Four gaps between a visit and a trial.

Each goal of the revamp started from a gap on the old website. Open a toggle to see what was wrong and what changed.

  1. 01

    Engagement & conversion

    Visitors weren’t guided toward trying the product.

    Current issues
    • The site structure didn’t lead visitors toward trying the service.
    • Calls-to-action weren’t prominent or engaging enough.
    Solutions
    • Clearer navigation flow from awareness to consideration to conversion.
    • Stronger CTAs with casual, action-led copy.
    • Live demo & video explainer so the benefit is clear in seconds.
  2. 02

    A brand that feels close

    The tone and visuals felt distant from the people it was selling to.

    Current issues
    • Tone of voice and visuals weren’t friendly enough for the target market.
    • The value of AI for business communication wasn’t highlighted.
    Solutions
    • Casual, persuasive language, referencing Gojek, Kammo, and Flip.
    • Fresher, more playful visuals relevant to each industry.
    • Real testimonials and case studies from active users.
  3. 03

    UX & SEO best practices

    The site was slower and harder to find than it should be.

    Current issues
    • Not fully optimized for SEO or loading speed.
    • The information structure could be simpler.
    Solutions
    • Speed optimization: compressed images, a CDN, and leaner code.
    • Mobile-first design that stays comfortable on phones.
    • SEO keywords that match how users search.
  4. 04

    Easier decisions

    Not everyone understood how the AI would help their business.

    Current issues
    • Visitors didn’t immediately see how the AI works or what it does for them.
    • The benefits for different industries needed more explanation.
    Solutions
    • Industry use cases showing how Cekat.AI helps each business type.
    • Live chatbot demo on the homepage to try the AI right away.

03 · The audience

Two markets, one website.

Cekat.AI helps businesses manage customer communication more efficiently. The website had to speak to small teams that want simplicity and to large teams that want control.

SMEs (UMKM) & startups

  • Want faster replies without growing the team.
  • Need automation for repetitive questions.
  • Want to sell more without being online 24/7.

Industries: e-commerce & online shops, F&B, services & consulting

Mid-size to enterprise

  • Handle high chat volumes across channels.
  • Need AI customer service to work efficiently.
  • Want one omnichannel system to manage teams.

Industries: banking & finance, property & real estate, retail & consumer goods

Age
25–45: business owners, managers, and customer support leads.
Gender
Not gender-specific; leans toward owners and support managers.
Location
Indonesia, mainly Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, and Medan.
Tech level
From teams already using CRM and chatbots to SMEs just going digital.

Insights that shaped the design

  1. 01

    Relatable language

    Casual, easy-to-understand copy to reach SMEs.

  2. 02

    Low-risk acquisition

    Educate through business and SME communities, and offer a free trial or freemium plan.

  3. 03

    Features for each market

    Easy setup and a simple UI for SMEs; stronger analytics and CRM integration for enterprises.

04 · The design

Six pages, one clear path.

Every page has one job, and each one moves the visitor a step closer to a trial.

Homepage

A quick overview of what Cekat.AI offers, with a hero and relatable tagline, testimonials, client logos, and case studies.

Introduce Cekat.AI in an engaging way and invite users to explore.

Features

Each capability explained with clear visuals: the AI chatbot, omnichannel integration, and analytics & performance reports.

Help users see how each feature supports their business.

Pricing

Packages with a feature breakdown and a comparison table, a free trial to start, and custom plans for larger businesses.

Make it easy to pick the right package.

Careers

Open roles with requirements and how to apply, plus culture, values, and employee testimonials.

Attract strong talent and build a cohesive team.

Blog

Articles on AI, chatbots, and digital business: tips, business case studies, and technology trends, written to be SEO-friendly.

Build thought leadership and bring in organic traffic.

About Us

The company’s vision, mission, and values, team and founders, and awards, with a way to contact the team or book a demo.

Build trust with customers and investors.

05 · Reflection

What changed, and what’s next.

The revamp gave Cekat.AI a website that is more user-friendly, more engaging, and better built for conversion.

  • Users understand the benefits of Cekat.AI faster.
  • Engagement and user retention went up.
  • SEO and website performance improved.

Next steps

  1. 01

    Iterate with data

    Study heatmaps and user behavior to keep improving the UX.

  2. 02

    A/B test the copy

    Find the most effective CTAs and wording.

  3. 03

    Grow the content

    More blog posts, videos, and social proof to build brand awareness.
